Welcome

A city stay in Malé

Tourist Inn is a collection of three guesthouses located in Malé City, designed for business travellers, locals, expats, and tourists alike. They emphasize comfort, convenience, and authentic island hospitality. Each property is situated within walking distance of beaches, shops, and cafes, providing easy access to the city's amenities. Services include airport transfers and a 24/7 WhatsApp concierge for guest assistance. The guesthouses offer a range of rooms, including family options, and amenities such as kitchenettes and air conditioning, aiming for a convenient and comfortable stay.

Male City Location

Located in Malé City, Tourist Inn provides proximity to the capital's offices, markets, and transport links. Rasfannu Beach and the National Museum are within 1km, with various places of worship and parks also nearby.

Room Amenities

Rooms offer private bathrooms, air conditioning, and essential conveniences. Some feature private kitchenettes with dining areas, mini-bars, and coffee machines, supported by daily housekeeping and shared kitchen facilities.

Guest Services

A 24/7 WhatsApp concierge is available for guest needs, alongside services like paid shuttle transfers and laundry. The properties include a lounge and offer shared kitchen access.

Breakfast Options

Guests can enjoy a continental, Asian, or à la carte breakfast. The establishment is noted for providing a very good breakfast experience.

Getting there

Tourist Inn is situated in Malé City, approximately 4.0 km from Velana International Airport. An airport taxi service operates via the Sinamalé Bridge. A paid airport shuttle service is also available for guest convenience.

An airport taxi is inexpensive — typically MVR 60–100 (about $4–8) on the fixed fares, taking 10–20 minutes; the public airport shuttle bus costs even less. No ferry or speedboat is needed — the islands are connected to the airport by road.

Your questions

How do I get to Tourist Inn from Velana International Airport?

Airport taxi is available, connecting via the Sinamalé Bridge. The distance is approximately 4.0 km. The property also offers an airport shuttle service, available for an extra charge.

What room types are available at Tourist Inn?

Available room types include Superior Double Room and Deluxe Double Room, both accommodating two guests with a queen bed. Family rooms are also mentioned as available.

What are the breakfast options?

Tourist Inn offers a continental breakfast, an Asian breakfast, and an à la carte breakfast option. Breakfast is highlighted as very good.

Are there kitchen facilities in the rooms?

Yes, some rooms feature a private kitchenette equipped with dining area, kitchenware, electric kettle, microwave, and refrigerator. A shared kitchen is also available.

What is the Wi-Fi situation?

Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the property and in all rooms, making it easy to stay connected during your stay.

Is parking available?

The property provides parking facilities for guests.

Does Tourist Inn offer family rooms?

Yes, family rooms are available, catering to guests travelling with children.
